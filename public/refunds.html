Free trial

We offer a 1-day free trial. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, your subscription automatically starts and your payment method is charged the then-current price. You can cancel during the trial at no charge.

How to cancel

Cancel any time from the customer portal (Manage subscription) or by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL]. Cancellation stops future renewals; you keep access until the end of the current paid period.

Refunds

Because this is digital content delivered immediately, we generally do not provide refunds for partial periods once a billing period has started, except where required by law or at our discretion (e.g. billing errors or a service that did not work as described).

Your statutory right to cancel (UK/EU)

Consumers normally have 14 days to cancel a distance purchase. For digital content and services, this right can be lost once performance begins with your express consent and acknowledgement that you lose the right. At checkout you will be asked to consent to immediate access, which means you agree the 14-day cancellation right no longer applies once access begins. This does not affect refunds for faulty or misdescribed services.
