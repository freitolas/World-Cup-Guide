We keep this light. The Game uses browser storage to function and does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

What we store on your device

  • Local storage (not cookies): your picks, your "started playing" date, and your cookie/consent choice. This stays on your device; anonymous play never sends it to us.
  • Authentication (only if you sign in): Supabase stores a session token in your browser to keep you logged in. Strictly necessary for accounts.
  • Payment (only at checkout): Stripe may set cookies needed to process payment and prevent fraud on its checkout pages.

Third parties

  • Stripe — payment & fraud prevention.
  • Supabase — authentication.
  • Google Fonts — serves fonts (no cookies; may receive your IP).
