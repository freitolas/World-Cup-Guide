1. Playing without an account

You can play the entire group-stage game anonymously. Your picks are stored only in your browser (localStorage) on your device — we don't receive them and they aren't tied to any identity.

2. Data we collect (only if you create an account)

  • Email and name — to create your account and sync picks across devices. Authentication is handled by Supabase.
  • Picks — once signed in, your predictions may be saved to your account.
  • Entitlement — whether you hold the $5 knockout pass and its expiry.
  • Payment data — card details are entered directly with Stripe; we never see or store your card number. We receive confirmation that payment succeeded.
  • Marketing consent — only if you tick the (unticked-by-default) box; we record that you consented and when.

3. Why, and our legal bases (UK GDPR)

  • Run accounts and sync picks — contract.
  • Take payment and grant the knockout pass — contract.
  • Marketing emails — consent (opt-in; withdraw anytime via the unsubscribe link or by emailing us).
  • Security and abuse prevention — legitimate interests.

4. Processors we share data with

  • Supabase — authentication & database (account email, name, picks, entitlement).
  • Stripe — payment processing (stripe.com/privacy).

  • Google Fonts — serves the site's fonts (may receive your IP when fonts load).

We do not sell your personal data.
